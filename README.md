## The meme

GTA V came out in 2013. GTA VI arrived on 19 November 2026 — 13 years later. The joke is the wait itself: thirteen years, and the little ghost from make.fun didn't age a day, it just kept holding its coin.

The poster is hand-drawn in SVG (no photos, no downloaded art): a Vice City sunset over the beach, palm trees, and the make.fun ghost front and centre, holding up its magenta coin. The headline reads "13 YEARS LATER / GTA VI IS FINALLY HERE", with the tagline "Worth every coin."

`meme/poster.html` + `meme/poster.css` are the hand-drawn poster; `tools/capture.mjs` renders it to `meme/meme.png` (1024×1024) with headless Chrome. `main.ts` copies `POST.json` to the output directory, unchanged.
